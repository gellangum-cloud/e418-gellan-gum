# Gellan Gum (E418) Technical Reference

A practical technical reference covering Gellan Gum (E418), including its properties, functional differences between high-acyl and low-acyl grades, gel formation, suspension, dosage, and common applications.

## What is Gellan Gum?

Gellan Gum is a microbial polysaccharide used as a gelling, stabilizing, suspending, and texturizing agent. It is widely used in food, beverages, biotechnology, and plant tissue culture.

Gellan Gum is also known as food additive E418 and has the CAS number 71010-52-1.

## High-Acyl and Low-Acyl Gellan Gum

Gellan Gum is commonly classified into high-acyl (HA) and low-acyl (LA) types.

High-acyl gellan gum generally produces softer, more elastic gels and is commonly used in plant-based beverages, dairy alternatives, and suspension systems.

Low-acyl gellan gum produces firmer and more brittle gels and is widely used in clear beverages, fruit preparations, structured gels, and plant tissue culture.
